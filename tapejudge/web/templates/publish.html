What freezing guarantees

  • The rule cannot be changed after this point.
  • Anyone can re-run the score for free.
  • Submitted judge scores cannot be edited.

What freezing does not guarantee

  • That judges scored fairly or independently.
  • That the criteria you chose are the right ones.
  • That an entry is original or a score is deserved.

TapeJudge makes the rule fixed, the acting identity verifiable, and the arithmetic reproducible. Bias and collusion are human problems it does not solve — it makes the inputs and the identities auditable so a dispute is about facts, not arithmetic.
