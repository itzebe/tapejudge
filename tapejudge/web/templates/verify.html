What this proves, and what it does not

Proves: given these committed scores, this is the ranking, and the rule could not have changed. Each score records the account that submitted it, bound to the judge identifier it scores under; the verified-account count is shown honestly. Does not prove: that the scores were fair or that the judges were independent. Those are human problems; TapeJudge makes the acting identity and the input auditable and the output reproducible.
