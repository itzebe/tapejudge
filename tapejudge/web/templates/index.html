How this works

An organiser writes a rubric; TapeJudge compiles it into a circuit and records it. Every score and every ranking is computed by that circuit, and anyone can re-run it for free. The rule cannot be edited after it is frozen; a judge's submitted score cannot be edited either. What the circuit does not prove is that the scores were fair — it makes the rule fixed and the arithmetic reproducible, and says so plainly.
